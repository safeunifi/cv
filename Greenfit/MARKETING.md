**Title:** PSA: Most swing faults are body limitations, not technique problems

> Been down a rabbit hole on TPI (Titleist Performance Institute) methodology lately and it's changed how I think about swing instruction.
>
> Short version: most swing "faults" like early extension, hip sway, casting, etc. are your body's compensation patterns for physical limitations — tight hips, weak glutes, restricted thoracic rotation.
>
> You can't fix the swing without fixing the body.
>
> I built a small app called Greenfit that records your swing, identifies your faults, then generates a targeted workout (and drill plan) for the physical root causes. Home-friendly, no equipment needed.
>
> Curious if others have found this TPI approach helpful? And what swing faults are you struggling with — I'd love to know if my fault → exercise mapping is on point.
